# v2 — Density: airy hero-stacked

## What makes v2 distinct
Splits the hero in two vertical acts. **Act 1 (top stage):** big Cormorant
headline + phone + two primary CTAs — a classic product-marketing hero.
**Act 2 (below):** the audience-specific pitch as a two-card row, one
card per audience.

This is the same information as v1, restaged. Above the fold you see one
message — "here's what AIRA is, here's what to do next" — and the
audience-specific detail becomes a scroll-reward.

## Key moves
- **Left-aligned copy stage** (eyebrow + headline + lede + sub + 2 CTAs)
  paired with a right-aligned phone. Feels like a modern SaaS hero
  without abandoning the brand cream/serif.
- **Two-card row below**, cards of equal width. Users card ends with a
  QR + store chips; Owners card ends with a full-width primary + outline
  button pair.
- **Card lift on hover** (translate + shadow) — a small interactive cue
  the reference lacks entirely.
- **Halo behind the phone** (soft radial olive at 20%) — replaces the
  ornamental leaves for a cleaner treatment.
- **CTA-first eyebrow copy** — subline is 46ch max so it never sprawls.

## Trade-offs
- Requires scroll for both audiences to see their pitch. Fine on
  desktop (single scroll), a real cost on mobile (~3 screens down to
  reach the Owners card).
- Loses the "we speak to both of you equally" symmetry the reference
  established.
- The above-the-fold headline is heavier, so the phone is smaller than
  in v1 to compensate.

## Best for
Landing-page conversion instincts — one message, one primary action,
detail on scroll. Good if analytics show most first-time users bounce
before scrolling and the top CTA is the goal.
