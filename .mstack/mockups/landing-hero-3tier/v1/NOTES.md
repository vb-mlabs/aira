# v1 — Hierarchy: balanced tri-column

## What makes v1 distinct
This is the direct read of the reference. Three equal-width columns with
the phone plate centered; centered headline sits above all three columns
and reads as a single title for the whole spread. User and Owner columns
get equivalent visual weight (same 68px round icon, same 28px Cormorant
title, same blurb width) so neither audience feels demoted.

## Key moves
- **Symmetric grid** (`1fr | 320-360px | 1fr`) — pure symmetry, phone as
  fixed-width center of gravity.
- **Headline placement is above, not beside** — matches the reference,
  gives the phone room to breathe without competing text.
- **Ornamental leaves** flanking the phone (olive + burnt-orange) —
  small brand nod that keeps the center from feeling too "product page".
- **Bullet list style** for users (checkmarks) and **benefit list** for
  owners (icon + strong + span) — mirrors the reference exactly.
- **Two-QR strip + two store badges** stack below the user bullets, in the
  same rhythm as the reference.

## Trade-offs
- Highest density of the three. Above the fold on a 1280 desktop you get
  everything: headline + phone + full user column + full owner column.
  Reads faster for people who know what they want, harder for first-timers.
- The two audience columns fighting for attention is a real cost. Mitigated
  by the centered headline binding them, but still — no clear next click.
- Mobile stack works but is long (~4 full screens on 375px).

## Best for
The client who liked the reference and wants that exact shape, refined
against AIRA's tokens.
