# v1 — Hybrid balanced (recommended primary)

**Hierarchy:** Editorial hero, then balanced shift to product showcase, then business owner panel, then footer. Email capture lives in the hero — first thing the visitor can do.

**Sections (in order):**

1. **Sticky nav** — tree-of-life logo + "AIRA by Nisarga" wordmark left; minimal nav links (For businesses, About) and "Get notified" CTA pill right.
2. **Editorial hero** — Cormorant headline "Find your community in Atlanta", italic tagline "Roots & Reach", brass-gold hairline divider, single paragraph in Lato, email input + "Get notified" button inline. Tree-of-life logo at moderate scale, offset to the side. Generous whitespace, paper texture visible.
3. **About AIRA** — 2-column editorial layout: left column Cormorant heading + Lato body explaining the curation thesis; right column inset frame highlighting the "Curated · Verified · Trusted" trinity.
4. **Categories preview** — 7-tile grid showing the actual Atlanta categories (Restaurants, Education, Events & Entertainment, Professional Services, Health & Wellness, Real Estate, Shopping) using the green/orange/brown tier color system. Each tile shows the hierarchy badge color.
5. **Phone mockup showcase** — Home + Listings screen PNGs side-by-side in a tilted device frame. Editorial copy on the right: "Here's what's coming to Atlanta."
6. **Trust 3-column** — "Curated by hand" / "Verified by community" / "Operated by Nisarga" with tree-of-life or check icons; small Lato body under each.
7. **For business owners** — distinct background (soft olive overlay), 2-col: copy on left ("List your business where Atlanta's Indian community looks"), business listing card preview on right (mock card matching the design system pattern). CTA email capture for businesses.
8. **Footer** — brand block + tagline + Contact Us icons + "Operated by Nisarga Group LLC" small print.

**Why it works:**
- Honors the brand's editorial soul in the hero but doesn't make you scroll past three sections to find the conversion ask.
- App preview is mid-page where intent is highest, not buried.
- Business owner panel is distinct visually (olive bg) so it doesn't compete with the end-user copy above.

**Tradeoffs:**
- The mid-page phone showcase will require updated app screens as the actual product evolves — there's maintenance debt.
- Email capture in the hero risks low-quality leads (people who didn't read the proposition before submitting).

**What I'd test post-launch if this is the winner:**
- Whether moving email capture from hero to a separate mid-page section lifts intent of captured leads.
- Whether the categories grid drives more "this is for me" recognition vs the phone mockup.
