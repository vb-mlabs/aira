Why we built this

The opposite of an algorithm and a pile of scraped data.

Most directory apps are an algorithm. AIRA isn't. Every listing on AIRA is reviewed by a person before it appears — real businesses, run by real people, sorted by who the community actually trusts.

We started in Atlanta because that's where Nisarga began. We're staying small on purpose. Trusted neighborhoods take time to build, and the Indian community in Atlanta is one of those neighborhoods. The dosa place, the tabla teacher, the mandap rental, the immigration lawyer who actually picks up the phone — the people you'd ask a friend about before you Googled it.

That's the bar. If a business doesn't clear it, it doesn't go on AIRA. And if it does, we tell you why — with a verified badge, a category that makes sense, and a tap-to-call number that actually works.

Atlanta first. Other cities, only when we can do them this well.
