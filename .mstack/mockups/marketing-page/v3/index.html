Why we built this

An algorithm and a pile of scraped data is not what this community needs.

Most directory apps are an algorithm and a pile of scraped data. They're built to scale to ten million businesses, none of which the person searching has any relationship with.

AIRA is the opposite. Every listing on AIRA is reviewed by a person before it appears — real businesses, run by real people, sorted by who the community actually trusts.

Atlanta first. Other cities, only when we can do them this well.

We started in Atlanta because that's where Nisarga began. We're staying small on purpose. Trusted neighborhoods take time to build, and the Indian community in Atlanta is one of those neighborhoods. The dosa place, the tabla teacher, the mandap rental, the immigration lawyer who actually picks up the phone — the people you'd ask a friend about before you Googled it.

That's the bar. If a business doesn't clear it, it doesn't go on AIRA. And if it does, we tell you why — with a verified badge, a category that makes sense, and a tap-to-call number that actually works on the first try.

We're not building a marketplace. We're stewarding a directory. Slow, careful, and one community at a time.
