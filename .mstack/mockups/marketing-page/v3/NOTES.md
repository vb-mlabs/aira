# v3 — Pure Magazine (contrast reference, not a candidate)

**Purpose:** Show what "lean all the way into the editorial brand register, let conversion suffer" looks like, so v1 and v2 can be evaluated against a clear anchor. This is NOT recommended as the shipping page — it's a creative reference point.

**Hierarchy:** Brand expression maximal. No phone mockups anywhere. Email capture present but subtle, in the footer. Reads like the homepage of a quarterly cultural publication, not a software product.

**Sections (in order):**

1. **Tiny nav** — logo small, single "Contact" text link right.
2. **Hero** — tree-of-life logo at hero-poster scale, single Cormorant sentence below ("AIRA. A directory of Atlanta's Indian community, curated with care."), nothing else. No CTA, no form, no scroll cue.
3. **Long-form essay** — 4-5 paragraphs of editorial copy in 2-column layout (or single column with very narrow measure). Drop caps. Pull quotes inline. Brass-gold hairlines as section dividers.
4. **Categories editorial layout** — categories as an editorial roster, large, generously spaced. No grid lines, no tiles — more like a magazine's "Sections" page.
5. **Nisarga mission statement** — full editorial block, Cormorant subhead "Why we built this", paragraph explaining the parent brand + community-stewardship angle.
6. **Footer** — brand mark, social, contact, AND the email signup hidden small at the bottom as "Notify me when AIRA launches" — a one-line input + arrow icon, no button copy.

**Why it works as a brand expression:**
- Maximum cohesion with the editorial aesthetic the design system encodes.
- Communicates seriousness and care — feels like a cultural institution, not a startup.
- Almost certainly the most beautiful of the three.

**Why it doesn't work as a shipping page:**
- Conversion will be measurably worse. Pre-launch we need email signups; this design buries the ask.
- New visitors won't immediately understand "this is an app you can download" — the connection to the mobile product is implicit, not explicit.
- App preview being entirely absent means visitors who *would* convert based on seeing the product can't.

**Use this variant to:**
- Ground discussions about how much brand expression v1 and v2 are willing to trade for conversion.
- Borrow specific moments (the long-form essay framing, the categories-as-editorial-roster) into the winning variant if they land.
