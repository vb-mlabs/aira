# V2 — Editorial Sidebar Split

**Concept:** A two-column layout where a fixed left sidebar lists all 7 categories as text links, and the right column shows the active category's businesses plus featured highlights at the top. Feels like a magazine or newspaper directory.

**What makes V2 distinct:**
- Category navigation always visible without scrolling — power-user friendly
- Editorial hierarchy: featured businesses get a wider card at the top of the right column
- Sidebar collapses to an off-canvas drawer on mobile (hamburger); main content goes full-width
- More information density; suits users who switch categories frequently

**Best for:** Return visitors who know the app and want fast category switching without navigating away.
